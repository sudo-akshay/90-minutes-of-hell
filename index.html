All-play table

The league as if every manager played everyone else each gameweek: a win for outscoring them, a draw for matching them. A huge week still counts as one set of wins, so this rewards steady scoring. Compare it with the real table to see who is riding a few big weeks.

Titles

One title for every manager, earned by whatever sets their game apart from the rest of the league. No two managers share a title, and they are recalculated on every refresh, so expect them to change as the season goes on.

Where the points come from

Points from goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards, with captain bonus counted in the captain's position. Bars share one scale, so a longer bar means more points.

Position points are before transfer hits, so a row can add up to more than the league table total.

Captain regret

Each gameweek, the captain's bonus points against the best captain the manager could have picked from their own starting XI, after auto-subs. Triple Captain weeks count the bonus twice.
